Privacy Policy
Battery & Charging Monitor • Effective date: November 19, 2026
Summary

Battery & Charging Monitor helps you inspect battery status, measure charging speed and cables, and manage charging alerts. Almost all activity remains stored locally on your phone, with no forced accounts, invasive ad tracking, or hidden analytics.

The only optional public feature is Global Rank. If you choose to tap "Upload your result on global", your selected nickname, device name, hardware specs, charger type, and measured current are published on the Rank board. Nothing is shared unless you trigger it yourself.

1. Who we are

Battery & Charging Monitor is a mobile and web diagnostic utility designed to give everyday phone users clear power, thermal, and charger insights without clutter.

2. Information we do not collect
  • No account registration, passwords, phone numbers, contacts, or personal identity profiles are required.
  • No GPS location trails, photos, media files, microphone recordings, or camera captures are accessed.
  • No advertising identifiers or third-party behavioral ad trackers are embedded in this app.
  • Private charging logs and settings stay on your device unless you explicitly upload a Rank benchmark.
3. Data used on your device

To display accurate diagnostics, the app reads standard battery and device attributes exposed by the platform (such as charge level, charging state, voltage, current, temperature, capacity, cycles, brand, model, and OS version).

  • Display preferences (theme choice, temperature unit °C/°F, sampling interval, orbit toggle, and screen wake option).
  • Recent charging session entries and sample history kept for quick review.
  • Your saved rating preference and last used Rank nickname.
4. Global Rank & optional sharing

Viewing rankings is optional and depends on your choice to share results. When you submit a score, the following fields can be included:

FieldHow it is provided
NicknameEntered by you before uploading your test result.
Device namePre-filled with your device name and editable prior to upload.
Brand & ModelDetected automatically from device hardware metadata.
OS & CapacityAndroid version and rated battery capacity (mAh) when available.
Charger & mASelected charger category along with average and peak mA readings.
5. Permissions & system capabilities
CapabilityPurpose
Battery telemetryReads live battery level, charging state, voltage, current, and temperature.
Screen wake lockKeeps the screen awake during active tests when enabled in Settings.
Local storageSaves your settings, history logs, and ratings locally on your device.
Network (optional)Used only when viewing or submitting optional Global Rank entries.
6. Changes & contact

We may revise this policy periodically as features evolve; significant updates will be reflected here with a revised effective date.

For questions or data requests, reach out via your app support channel or email.
